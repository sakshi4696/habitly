FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------
Habitly is a simple habit tracker for people who just want to check things off and see how they're doing — no clutter, no sign-ups, no ads.

Track any habit you want — reading, workouts, drinking water, meditating, whatever it is for you. Mark it done for the day with one tap, and Habitly quietly keeps count in the background.

WHAT YOU GET
- Daily view: see today's habits, and swipe through past or future days
- Streaks: know exactly how many days in a row you've kept a habit going
- Stats: see your completion rate at a glance
- Progress view: a week, month, or year calendar so you can spot patterns
- Edit or delete habits anytime — nothing is locked in

Everything is stored right on your phone. No account, no login, no internet connection required — Habitly works completely offline and never sends your data anywhere.

If Habitly is helping you build better habits, a quick rating would mean a lot — it helps other people find the app too.
