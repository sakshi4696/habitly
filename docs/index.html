Privacy Policy for Habitly

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Habitly is a simple habit and streak tracking app. This page explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. Habitly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Every habit you add and every day you mark as done is saved locally on your own device only. This data never leaves your phone and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Accounts and sign-in

Habitly does not require an account or sign-in of any kind. We have no way to identify you as a user.

Third-party services

Habitly does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only feature that talks to an outside service is the "Rate the app" button, which opens Google Play's own review prompt or the Habitly Play Store page. That interaction is handled entirely by Google Play and is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Data sharing

Since we don't collect any data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to anyone.

Data deletion

All your data lives only on your device. Uninstalling Habitly permanently deletes all of it. There is no separate copy stored anywhere else to request deletion of.

Children's privacy

Habitly does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13 — because it doesn't collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Habitly adds a feature that does collect data — this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
